138   <para>
139    The operators <literal>AND</literal> and <literal>OR</literal> are
140    commutative, that is, you can switch the left and right operands
141    without affecting the result.  (However, it is not guaranteed that
142    the left operand is evaluated before the right operand.  See <xref
143    linkend="syntax-express-eval"/> for more information about the
144    order of evaluation of subexpressions.)
