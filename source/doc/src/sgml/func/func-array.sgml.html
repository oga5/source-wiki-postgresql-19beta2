2  <title>Array Functions and Operators</title>
3
4  <para>
5   <xref linkend="array-operators-table"/> shows the specialized operators
6   available for array types.
7   In addition to those, the usual comparison operators shown in <xref
8   linkend="functions-comparison-op-table"/> are available for
9   arrays.  The comparison operators compare the array contents
10   element-by-element, using the default B-tree comparison function for
11   the element data type, and sort based on the first difference.
12   In multidimensional arrays the elements are visited in row-major order
13   (last subscript varies most rapidly).
14   If the contents of two arrays are equal but the dimensionality is
15   different, the first difference in the dimensionality information
16   determines the sort order.
17  </para>
